# Status update template

Use these sections in this order. Keep each to a few bullets, and leave out a section with nothing to report.

```markdown
## <Project name>: update for <period>

**Summary.** One or two sentences: overall progress against the project's goal, and anything that needs attention.

**Completed**
- <Task title> (<ref>), completed by <name>

**In progress**
- <Task title> (<ref>): <what's happening, from its status and recent changes>

**Blocked or at risk**
- <Task title> (<ref>): <why it's blocked, or that it's overdue since <date>>

**Decisions**
- <Decision recorded in the project's decisions or comments during the period>

**Next**
- <From the project's next actions and the highest-priority open tasks>
```

Write plainly for someone who hasn't been following closely. Use names as MCPBinder shows them. Say "overdue since <date>" rather than "late".
